The flag is used as a boolean and its text is not rendered; nothing is lost, since sqladmin only ever sets it to "Invalid credentials.". What was missing was not precision but truth. The first version of that fix put the lockout policy in the alert box as a second paragraph, and the owner read it on the FIRST wrong password as "I am locked out now". Present tense, full-size, on the alarm ground, and directly under the one line that reports the actual outcome, a standing rule about the future reads as a diagnosis of the present.
