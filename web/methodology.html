What we record

The sector, food category, the specific food where you name one, and the quantities entered into the calculator are recorded anonymously. They join the privacy-protected public statistics only if you choose to offer them. We do not record information that identifies you or your business.

Your browser also keeps a copy of your answers, and of the result you were last shown, so that following a link and pressing Back does not throw the calculation away. That copy belongs to the tab you are using: it is not shared with your other tabs, it is never sent to us or to anyone else, and it ends when you close the tab. The calculator's Clear all data button removes it straight away, and the calculator still works in a browser that refuses to keep anything at all. A result brought back this way is the one you already saw, shown again with the factors that produced it rather than worked out again.
