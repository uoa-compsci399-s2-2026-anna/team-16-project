Turn food waste information into action

Estimate the environmental and financial impact of food waste, then explore how changes to its destination could affect the result.

Use the calculator

Your entries are submitted anonymously when you calculate results.

What you will need

  • Where the waste occurred in the food supply chain
  • The food category, if known
  • The total waste amount — a weight, or how many containers you fill
  • How that total was distributed across waste destinations
