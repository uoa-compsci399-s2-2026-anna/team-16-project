What this page is for

This runs one line through the real calculator, against whichever version of the numbers you choose, and shows you what comes back. The request is marked as a dry run, so nothing is written down: no calculation is stored, and nothing you do here reaches the public statistics. Run it as many times as tuning a formula takes.

It is also the only way to watch a draft's numbers work. The public calculator only ever uses the published set, so until you publish, this page and Compare with published are the only places a draft can be seen running.

Do not test on the public calculator instead. Every calculation a visitor runs is counted, and those counts are what the public statistics page reports on the client's behalf. Twenty runs while you tune a formula are twenty calculations that never happened, sitting inside a figure that may be quoted in public — and nobody can take them out again afterwards, because the calculator deliberately stores nothing that would say which were yours.
