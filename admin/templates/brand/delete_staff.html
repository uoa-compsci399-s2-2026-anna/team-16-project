What this does, and what it does not

  • The account is removed outright, along with its authenticators and its recovery codes. Nobody can log in as it again, and the username becomes free for somebody else to use.
  • Everything the account did stays in the audit log, in full, still naming it. The trail records who made each change as a name rather than as a link to this list, so deleting an account never makes an entry stop saying who was responsible. Deleting is not a way to remove a record of anything.
  • An account must be deactivated first. Deactivating is the step you can undo — Reactivate on the accounts screen puts the account back exactly as it was. This one you cannot.
  • You cannot delete your own account, and the panel will not go below two administrators who can log in.
